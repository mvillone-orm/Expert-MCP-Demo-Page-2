## Technical Executive
**Target Audience**: CTO, VP Engineering, VP Platform

### The Pitch
It connects your AI directly to named, independent technical experts.
So when you are pressure-testing a major architectural decision,
evaluating a build versus buy call, or trying to get ahead of an industry shift,
the output comes back grounded in practitioners who have done it,
not a generic recommendation you cannot stand behind.
It works like an advisory layer in your tech stack at the moment you need it,
not weeks later, when the decision is already made.

## Learning and Development (LandD)
**Target Audience**: Director of LandD, VP Talent Development, CHRO

### The Pitch
Teams are adopting AI tools rapidly to write code faster.
But writing code fast does not mean writing code that is defensible or correct.
When developers rely on generic LLMs, they copy unverified outputs without learning.
O'Reilly Expert MCP grounds your AI workflows in verified, expert-backed content,
ensuring that your engineers are actually learning from practitioners,
and producing secure, high-quality code that you can stand behind.
It provides the verification and data to justify your skills strategy and ROI to stakeholders.
